Durga Puja is more than just a festival for Bengalis across the globe — it is an emotion, a sensory memory of autumn skies, the resonance of Dhak, and the magic of walking through night-lit Kolkata alleys.

To celebrate this spirit, I built **বোধন (Bodhon) — Durga Puja 2026**: A cinematic Bengali editorial web application and intelligent city guide designed to help festival lovers discover pandals, find nearest metro or bus stations, and get transit directions.

---

### 🎨 The Design Philosophy: "শিউলি সকাল" (Morning Shiuli)
Instead of building another generic SaaS directory, Bodhon was crafted as a digital ritual invitation:
Warm paper & cream textures (`#F8F1E4`, `#FFF9EF`) reflecting traditional puja cards.
Bodhon Vermilion (`#B52A22`) & Marigold Yellow (`#E5A62C`) celebrating ritual sacredness and energy.
Bilingual Editorial Typography: Authentic **Noto Serif Bengali** for poetic headlines paired with **Manrope** for modern interface controls.

---

### 💡 What Makes Bodhon Special?

1. 🔍 **Instant Pandal Search & Discovery**:
Debounced search filtering through 70+ iconic pandals (North to South Kolkata and beyond) by name, neighborhood (Bagbazar, Sovabazar, Ekdalia, Tridhara), or category.

2. 🗺️ **Dual-Layer Interactive Map & Transit Intelligence**:
Integrated with Google Maps API and a custom illustrated canvas fallback. With one click on "Use my location", Bodhon automatically sorts pandals by distance and highlights the nearest **Kolkata Metro station** (Blue/Green lines) and **Bus stop** with walking legs!

3. 📻 **Bodhon Radio (Spotify-like Audio Glider)**:
A ceremonial music player featuring celebratory Agomoni tracks with real-time scrubbers and an animated vinyl/alpona gramophone reel powered by GSAP.

4. ⏳ **Real-Time Mahalaya Countdown & Day-by-Day Tithi Calendar**:
Live countdown to Mahalaya dawn (October 10, 2026) with detailed ritual day notes from Maha Panchami to Vijaya Dashami.

5. 👥 **Live Festive Visitor Counter**:
A celebratory visitor badge in the footer honoring everyone celebrating together.

---

### 🛠️ Built With Modern Web Technologies:
- **Frontend**: React 19, Vite 7, TailwindCSS v4, Wouter, Radix UI Primitives
- **Animations**: GSAP (ScrollTrigger) & Framer Motion
- **Backend & APIs**: Express 4, tRPC v11, Drizzle ORM, Google Maps JavaScript API
- **State Management**: Zustand & TanStack React Query
- **Testing**: Vitest & JSDOM
